CAR GUY KNOWLEDGE

Numbers are easy.
Understanding is rare.

Short, useful automotive explainers that help you walk into the showroom with better questions.

01

TORQUE

Torque is the twisting force produced by an engine or motor. More torque generally means stronger low-speed pull, making acceleration, overtaking, climbing and carrying passengers easier.

UNDERSTAND →
02

FWD / RWD / AWD

FWD sends power to the front wheels, RWD to the rear wheels, while AWD can distribute power across all four. The difference affects traction, handling, stability and driving feel.

UNDERSTAND →
03

ADAS

Advanced Driver Assistance Systems use cameras, radar and sensors to help with functions such as automatic emergency braking, lane assistance, adaptive cruise control and blind-spot monitoring.

UNDERSTAND →
04

PETROL / DIESEL / EV

Petrol engines are usually smooth and responsive, diesels are known for strong torque and efficiency, while EVs deliver instant torque with quiet, efficient electric propulsion. The right choice depends on how you actually drive.
